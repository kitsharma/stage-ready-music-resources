# Immersive environment assets

The Concert Hall and Recording Studio plates are project-local generated images. They contain no interface text; accessible labels, focus states, lighting effects, and hit regions are implemented in HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

## Design provenance

- Stitch project: `11091067758089258234`
- Stage Ready design system: `assets/de8e759123a54cc5a30620fb2db3175f`
- Concert Hall Stitch source screen: `eb77f746786e457eb6f3c02a24a019f1`
- Refined Concert Hall Stitch screen: `c84e54170af34655a73fdaa7d8e02a26`
- Stitch's available fast models were used for spatial layout exploration. The final environment plates were generated with ChatGPT image generation after the Stitch outputs remained too UI-like.

## Final image prompts

### Concert Hall

Wide 16:9 architectural photograph of an empty concert hall stage from the centered audience/conductor viewpoint: crimson velvet proscenium, open wings, curved ribbed wooden acoustic shell, overhead truss, five amber spotlights through haze, polished perspective floor, central choral risers, recessed foreground orchestra pit, and sparse elementary-music instruments. No people, text, labels, cards, controls, logos, or watermarks.

### Recording Studio

Wide 16:9 architectural photograph from a warm professional control room: low walnut desk without controls, large acoustic-glass window into a live room with piano and classroom instruments, separate glass vocal booth on the left, open tape/archive alcove on the right, walnut acoustic panels, amber practical lighting, and restrained teal accents. No people, text, screens, cards, faders, controls, logos, or watermarks.
